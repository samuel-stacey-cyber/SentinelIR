# Questions for reviewers

[Back to overview](README.md)

I would appreciate feedback on the points below. Please answer whichever fit your experience; brief comments are welcome.

1. Does the CTF support the research questions, and is the scope achievable?
2. Is the core route suitable for students with different experience levels? Are the clues, decoys and optional challenges fair?
3. Are there any unintended shortcuts or technical issues I should consider?
4. Is using scripted activity for the main detection comparison, with student activity analysed separately, appropriate?
5. Are the response-plan tasks and scoring approach feasible? What would improve the assessment?
6. What hosting arrangement would you recommend? Are suitable university facilities available?
7. Are there recruitment, ethics or data-handling requirements I have missed, including for optional findings reports?
8. What would you change, remove or prioritise before development?

Hosting, technical integration and some study details remain open. Suggestions on simplifying the plan would be particularly useful.

## After feedback

I will record each suggestion, my decision and the reason, then update the plan before development. Feedback will inform the design; technical checks, pilot testing and formal evaluation will still be needed.
